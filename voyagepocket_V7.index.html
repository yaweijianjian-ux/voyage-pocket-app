Jazz Fest Barcelona

by @mira.k posted apr 9, 2026 📍 Poble-Sec

Three days, six venues, ninety performers. Barcelona's jazz scene takes over Poble-Sec with everything from bebop quartets to Brazilian fusion.

Last year I only caught the final night — this time I'm blocking out the whole weekend. The late-night jam sessions at Jamboree are worth the sleepless morning.

pro tip: the free outdoor stage at Plaça del Sortidor fills up by 7pm. Grab a sandwich from the Quimet & Quimet across the plaza, post up early.

Inside Sagrada Família

by @mira.k apr 9, 2026 📍 Barcelona, ES edited apr 11

You can read about Gaudí's basilica for hours and still not be prepared for the first time you look up inside it. The light hits the stone through stained glass and the whole interior turns into something that feels less built than grown.

The tree-like columns branch toward the ceiling. Gaudí tested the structure with upside-down chain models — gravity doing his math for him.

🎤 voice memo · 0:42

"so I just walked in and the light was hitting the stained glass perfectly — I had to stop talking for a second"

"You either get the crane in the photo or you don't — I've made peace with the crane."

Entry tickets: book online at least a week out. Audio guide: skip it for the nave, take it for the crypt. Best light: late afternoon, west windows.

Tapas night, barely

2026.04.10 📍 Barcelona, ES 🔒 only you

Barcelona bit me today. Four places in one neighborhood, two sangrias deep, and a waiter named Marc who said "tranquila" every time I apologized for my terrible Spanish.

The patatas bravas at Bar Pinotxo were perfect — crisp outside, molten in. I want to remember this always.

note to self: come back. different neighborhood next time.
felt really really happy walking home

Tomorrow: Park Güell in the morning if I can wake up.
